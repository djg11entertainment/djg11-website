DJ G11 PREMIUM WEBSITE — SETUP GUIDE

OPENING THE WEBSITE
Open index.html in a browser.

PUBLISHING
Upload the entire DJ_G11_Premium_Website folder contents to your host.
The file named index.html must stay at the website root.

1. HERO VIDEO
Add your real full-screen video here:
assets/media/hero-video.mp4

Recommended:
- 15–30 seconds
- Weddings and full dance floors
- 1920×1080 or 1280×720
- No audio needed
- Compress to under 12 MB when possible
- H.264 MP4

2. ACTUAL DJ PHOTOS
Replace:
assets/media/dj-photo-1.svg
assets/media/dj-photo-2.svg

You may use JPG, PNG, or WebP. If the file extension changes, update its filename in index.html.

3. PHOTO GALLERY
Replace gallery-1.svg through gallery-6.svg in assets/media.
WebP is recommended for speed.

4. VIDEO HIGHLIGHTS
Add:
assets/media/highlight-1.mp4
assets/media/highlight-2.mp4

5. MUSIC MIXES
Add:
assets/audio/wedding-mix.mp3
assets/audio/global-mix.mp3
assets/audio/graduation-mix.mp3

6. AUTOMATIC QUOTE EMAILS
Find this line in index.html:
action="https://formsubmit.co/YOUR-BOOKING-EMAIL"

Replace YOUR-BOOKING-EMAIL with your real email address.
Example:
action="https://formsubmit.co/bookings@djg11.com"

FormSubmit will send an activation email the first time the form is used.
For a more advanced CRM, replace this form with HoneyBook, Dubsado, Wix Forms, or Formspree.

7. LIVE AVAILABILITY
Open availability.js and add booked dates:
window.DJG11_UNAVAILABLE_DATES = [
  "2027-06-12",
  "2027-07-04"
];

This is a client-side checker. It does not read Google Calendar automatically.
For true real-time calendar synchronization, connect HoneyBook, Calendly, Dubsado,
or create a secure Google Calendar API backend.

8. PROFESSIONAL BOOKING CALENDAR
Replace the visual calendar in index.html with your Calendly, HoneyBook,
Dubsado, or Google appointment scheduling embed code.

9. REVIEWS
Replace all sample testimonial text with verified client reviews.
Update the Facebook and Google Review button URLs.

10. INSTAGRAM FEED
The website links directly to @djg11_1beat.
For an automatic live feed, use an Instagram feed provider such as Elfsight or LightWidget
and paste its embed into the social section.

11. GOOGLE MAP
The map currently centers on Kingsland, Georgia.
To change it, edit this part of index.html:
q=Kingsland%2C%20Georgia

12. PACKAGE PRICES
Replace “Custom quote” with actual starting prices, or keep custom quote.

13. FACEBOOK LINK
Replace each href="#" connected to Facebook with your full Facebook page URL.

14. SEO
The page already includes:
- Title tag
- Meta description
- Keywords
- Open Graph information
- Local-business structured data
- Semantic headings
- Mobile-friendly layout
- Lazy-loaded map
- Media preload controls

Before launch:
- Add your final website domain to the structured data
- Add a Google Business Profile
- Connect Google Search Console
- Submit sitemap.xml
- Add descriptive alt text to all real images

15. SPEED
For best loading speed:
- Convert photos to WebP
- Keep each photo under 300 KB where practical
- Keep hero video under 12 MB
- Avoid uploading full-length videos directly
- Use YouTube/Vimeo for longer highlights
- Enable CDN and caching through your web host
